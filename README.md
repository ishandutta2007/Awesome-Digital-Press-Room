# Awesome-Digital-Press-Room

# Top Digital Press Room Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Online Newsrooms, Media Kits, Press Release Distribution, Journalist Portals & Brand News Centers*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Digital Press Rooms**. These systems host official company news, media assets, and press contacts in a journalist-friendly online newsroom—often paired with wire distribution.

**Examples** include Presspage, Prezly, Prowly, Notified, Newswire, PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire, MediaHQ, and Pressat (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Dedicated digital press-room SaaS is mostly commercial. Open alternatives are **CMS and publishing stacks** (WordPress, Ghost, Superdesk) configured as media centers, plus static press-kit patterns. This section lists every significant relevant approach found.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Presspage, Prezly, Prowly](https://www.presspage.com/)**  
  Dedicated digital newsroom platforms—branded press rooms, media databases, and journalist engagement tools.

- **[PR Newswire, Business Wire, GlobeNewswire, Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/)**  
  Wire distribution networks often paired with online newsrooms for broad media reach.

- **[Notified, MediaHQ, Pressat](https://www.notified.com/)**  
  PR and media-room solutions for press release hosting, distribution, and coverage tracking.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[WordPress](https://github.com/WordPress/WordPress)**  
  Most common open foundation for DIY digital press rooms—custom post types for releases, media library, and journalist-facing themes.

- **[Ghost](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost)**  
  Open publishing platform well suited to a clean, modern company newsroom with memberships if needed.

- **[Superdesk](https://github.com/superdesk/superdesk)**  
  Open-source newsroom CMS for media organizations—end-to-end news production and distribution (heavier than a corporate press room, but relevant).

- **[We.Publish](https://github.com/wepublish/wepublish)**  
  Open headless CMS and peering network oriented toward independent media and shared publishing.

- **[Hugo / Eleventy static press sites](https://github.com/gohugoio/hugo)**  
  Open static-site generators ideal for fast, secure, low-maintenance press kits and news archives.

- **[Directus / Strapi headless CMS](https://github.com/directus/directus)**  
  Open headless CMS backends for custom journalist portals and media asset APIs.

- **[Matomo for newsroom analytics](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)**  
  Open analytics to measure press-room traffic without sending journalist data to third-party ad networks.

- **[Open media-kit templates](https://github.com/search?q=press+kit+OR+media+kit+template)**  
  Community HTML/PDF and site templates for logos, bios, and boilerplate.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Corporate newsroom**: WordPress or Ghost with a press-specific theme and media library.
- **Static & secure**: Hugo/Eleventy for simple, high-performance press pages.
- **Headless**: Strapi/Directus + custom frontend for tailored journalist UX.
- **Composable stacks**: CMS newsroom + wire service API + open analytics.
- Commercial press-room platforms still lead in media databases, embargo tools, and distribution networks.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**WordPress** or **Ghost** for the public newsroom; **static generators** for simple kits; optional **headless CMS** for apps.  
Wire distribution (PR Newswire, Business Wire, etc.) remains commercial.  
Dedicated SaaS (Presspage, Prezly, Prowly) adds journalist CRM and polish.  
Many companies run effective open CMS press rooms; global brands often combine CMS with commercial wires and DRP. Fully open digital press rooms are practical for hosting; distribution reach is the commercial gap.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Press materials can affect markets and reputation. Ensure accuracy, approvals, and disclosure. Journalist data in media databases is personal data—handle under applicable privacy law.
- Open CMS stacks offer control and cost efficiency but require you to manage security, design, and SEO. Commercial platforms shift product and media-contact features to the vendor. Neither replaces professional PR judgment.
